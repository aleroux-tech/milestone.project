Who I Am

My name is Amanda Leroux, and I'm a student at Campus University. I live in a small town in Oklahoma, and I'm a mom to five boys who mean everything to me. I've always loved technology and figuring things out. Anytime someone I know has a tech problem, they usually come straight to me because I’ve always been able to fix things.

Getting into web design has honestly been one of the best choices I've made. I enjoy learning how everything works behind the scenes and watching myself improve with every project. My goal is to keep learning and eventually turn this into a long‑term career.
