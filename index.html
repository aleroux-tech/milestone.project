Who I Am

Hello, my name is Amanda Leroux, and I am a student at Campus University. I live in a small town in Oklahoma, and I'm a mom to five amazing boys who mean the world to me. I've always loved technology for as long as I can remember. It's something that has always come naturally to me, and anytime someone I know has a tech problem, they usually come straight to me because I can figure things out.

Starting web design has honestly been one of the best decisions I've made. I absolutely love it. I enjoy learning how everything works behind the scenes and seeing my skills grow with every project. My goal is to keep learning as much as I can about web development and software and hopefully turn it into a long‑term career.

My IT Journey
